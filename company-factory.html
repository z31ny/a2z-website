So Good

Frozen & Processed Foods · Est. 2008

So Good is a 100% Egyptian food manufacturer founded in 2008. It began supplying restaurants and hotels, then moved into production in 2015 — combining great taste, high quality, and affordable prices under strict quality control. Its range spans processed meat, poultry, fish, ready shawarma skewers, and cheese.
