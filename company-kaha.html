Kaha Company for Preserved Foods

Canned & Preserved Foods · Est. 1940 · Kaha, Qalyubia

Egypt's first canned and preserved food company, established in 1940 in Kaha, Qalyubia. Today it operates seven factories across Qalyubia, Sharqeya, Alexandria, and Beheira, running 19 production lines. ISO 9001 certified, it is a subsidiary of the Holding Company for Food Industries under Egypt's Ministry of Supply.
